# Product Overview

## Introduction

SMOOCH is a compact AI-powered companion designed to create expressive and engaging interactions through personality, voice, and animation.

Rather than functioning as a conventional smart assistant, SMOOCH is designed as a character users can interact with and enjoy having nearby. The focus is on creating a fun, expressive experience that feels personal and entertaining.

---

## Key Features

- Expressive animated face
- Personality-driven interactions
- Voice-based conversations
- Compact desktop companion
- Rechargeable portable design
- Custom voice personalization

---

## Personalized Voice

One of SMOOCH's defining features is personalized voice.

Customers can choose to have their companion prepared using a custom voice sample that they provide with the appropriate permission. This allows every SMOOCH to feel unique and personally meaningful while preserving the product's own personality.

Details regarding the underlying voice generation technology are intentionally not included in this repository.

---

## Design Philosophy

SMOOCH is built around three principles:

- Personality before functionality
- Emotional interaction over utility
- Premium industrial design in a compact form factor

The goal is to create a companion that users enjoy interacting with rather than simply operating.

---

## Future Vision

The SMOOCH platform is intended to expand into multiple character personalities while sharing a common hardware platform.

Future versions may offer different appearances, personalities, and experiences while maintaining a consistent user experience.
